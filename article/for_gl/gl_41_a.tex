\begin{theorem} \label{thm:gl41-a}
  Let $a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $\lambda = (a,b,c,d|a)$. The projective objects $P_{\lambda}$ in $\mathfrak{B}_{b,c,d}$ have the following Verma flag formulae.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof} The Cases are as follows:\par
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $ a\neq \{b,c,d\} $
    \begin{enumerate}
      \item $ b<a<d<c,\; d>a+1 $\par
        Let $\mu:=\lambda + (1,0,0,0|0) $.
        \begin{equation}\notag
          \begin{aligned}
            Pr_{\lambda}(P_\mu \otimes V_{2}) &=
M_{a,b,c,d|a}
+ M_{a+1,b,c,d|a+1}
+ M_{d,c,b,a|a}
+ M_{d,c,b,a+1|a+1}
+ 2M_{c,d,a,b|a}
+ 2M_{c,d,a+1,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,a,b,d|a}
+ M_{c,a+1,b,d|a+1}
+ M_{a,c,d,b|a}
+ M_{a+1,c,d,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,b,d,a|a}
+ M_{c,b,d,a+1|a+1}
+ M_{d,a,c,b|a}
+ M_{d,a+1,c,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,d,b,a|a}
+ M_{c,d,b,a+1|a+1}
+ M_{d,c,a,b|a}
+ M_{d,c,a+1,b|a+1}
+ M_{a,c,b,d|a}
+ M_{a+1,c,b,d|a+1}
+ M_{d,b,c,a|a}
+ M_{d,b,c,a+1|a+1}
+ 2M_{c,a,d,b|a}
+ 2M_{c,a+1,d,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,b,a,d|a}
+ M_{c,b,a+1,d|a+1}
+ M_{a,d,c,b|a}
+ M_{a+1,d,c,b|a+1} \\
                                               &=
\sum M_{a,b,c,d|a}
+ \sum M_{a+1,b,c,d|a+1}
+ \sum M_{c,a,d,b|a}
+ \sum M_{c,a+1,d,b|a+1}
          \end{aligned}
        \end{equation}
      Similarly, the set of indeterminate composition factors is $U = \{
M_{c,d,a,b|a},
M_{c,d,a+1,b|a+1},
M_{c,a,d,b|a},
M_{c,a+1,d,b|a+1}
\}$. 

From \cref{tab:verma-analysis_41_a_1}, \cref{prop:1} alone is insufficient to rule out $P_{c,d,a,b|a}$ or $P_{c,a,d,b|a}$ as direct summands of $\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_{2})$.

We therefore invoke the complete standard filtrations of these projective modules. By \cref{thm:gl41-c} and \cref{thm:gl41-b} (whose proofs are independent of the present argument, thus avoiding circular reasoning), we have
\begin{equation}\notag
  \begin{aligned}
    P_{c,d,a,b|a} = M_{c,d,a+1,b|a+1}
+M_{c,d,a,b|a}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\notag
  \begin{aligned}
    P_{c,a,d,b|a} &=
M_{c,d,a+1,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,d,a,b|a}
+ M_{c,a+1,d,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,a,d,b|a} \\
                  &=
\sum M_{c,a,d,b|a}
+ \sum M_{c,a+1,d,b|a+1}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Since all Verma modules appearing in $\Filt(P_{c,d,a,b|a})$ and $\Filt(P_{c,a,d,b|a})$ belong to the multiset $U$, and their multiplicities do not exceed those in $U$, filtration comparison alone cannot exclude either $P_{c,d,a,b|a}$ or $P_{c,a,d,b|a}$ as direct summands of $\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_{2})$. We proceed by contradiction to eliminate both possibilities.


First, assume that $P_{c,d,a,b|a}$ is a direct summand of $\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_{2})$. Then there exists a projective module $Q$ such that
\begin{equation}\notag
  \begin{aligned}
    \prl(P_\lambda \otimes V_{2}) = Q \oplus P_{c,d,a,b|a}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Let $\nu = (a+2, b, c, d | a+1)$. Under our standing assumptions on the parameters, $\nu$ is a typical weight. We divide the argument into two cases.

Case 1. $c>a+2$.

A direct computation yields the standard filtration of $\pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_1)$ as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gl44-1}
  \begin{aligned}
    \pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_{1}) &= 
    M_{a+2,b,c,d|a+1}
+ M_{d,c,b,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{c,d,a+2,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,a+2,b,d|a+1}
+ M_{a+2,c,d,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,b,d,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{d,a+2,c,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,d,b,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{d,c,a+2,b|a+1}
+ M_{a+2,c,b,d|a+1}
+ M_{d,b,c,a+2|a+1}
+ 2M_{c,a+2,d,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,b,a+2,d|a+1}
+ M_{a+2,d,c,b|a+1} \\
                             &=
\sum M_{a+2,b,c,d|a+1}
+ M_{c,a+2,d,b|a+1}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Observe that $\nu$ is the minimal highest weight occurring in this filtration. Since $Q$ is projective, so is $\pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_1)$. By \cref{lemma:2}, this implies that $P_\nu$ must appear as a direct summand.

However, \cref{thm:4} gives the complete standard filtration of $P_\nu$ as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gl44-2}
  \begin{aligned}
    P_\nu &= M_{a+2,b,c,d|a+1}
+ M_{d,c,b,a+2|a+1}
+ 2M_{c,d,a+2,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,a+2,b,d|a+1}
+ M_{a+2,c,d,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,b,d,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{d,a+2,c,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,d,b,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{d,c,a+2,b|a+1}
+ M_{a+2,c,b,d|a+1}
+ M_{d,b,c,a+2|a+1}
+ 2M_{c,a+2,d,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,b,a+2,d|a+1}
+ M_{a+2,d,c,b|a+1} \\
                             &=
\sum M_{a+2,b,c,d|a+1}
+ \sum M_{c,a+2,d,b|a+1}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Comparing \cref{eq:gl44-2} with \cref{eq:gl44-2}, we see that the Verma module $M_{c,d,a+2,b|a+1}$ appears with multiplicity one in $\Filt(\pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_1))$, but with multiplicity two in $\Filt(P_\nu)$. Consequently, $P_\nu$ cannot be a direct summand of $\pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_1)$, contradicting \cref{lemma:2}. Hence, $P_{c,d,a,b|a}$ is not a direct summand of $\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_2)$.

Case 2. $d=a+2$.

 直接计算$\pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_1)$ 标准滤过如下
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gl44-4}
  \begin{aligned}
    \pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_{1}) &= 
2M_{a+2,b,c,a+2|a+1}
+2M_{a+2,c,b,a+2|a+1}
+3M_{c,a+2,a+2,b|a+1}
+2M_{c,a+2,b,a+2|a+1}
+2M_{a+2,c,a+2,b|a+1}
+2M_{c,b,a+2,a+2|a+1}
+2M_{a+2,a+2,c,b|a+1}
                             &=
2\sum M_{a+2,b,c,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{c,a+2,a+2,b|a+1}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}

通过公式\cref{eq:gl44-4},可以看出$\nu$是$\Filt(\pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_{1}))$中的极小权，且$M_\nu$重数是2。这说明$\pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_{1})$中有两个$P_\nu$作为其分解的直和项。
但是，\cref{thm:4} 给出的$P_\nu$的标准滤过是
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gl44-5}
  \begin{aligned}
    P_\nu &= 
M_{a+2,b,c,a+2|a+1}
+M_{a+2,c,b,a+2|a+1}
+2M_{c,a+2,a+2,b|a+1}
+M_{c,a+2,b,a+2|a+1}
+M_{a+2,c,a+2,b|a+1}
+M_{c,b,a+2,a+2|a+1}
+M_{a+2,a+2,c,b|a+1} \\
                 &=
\sum M_{a+2,b,c,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{c,a+2,a+2,b|a+1}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
比较\cref{eq:gl44-4}和\cref{eq:gl44-5}，我们可知$\pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_{1})$不可能有两个$P_\nu$作为其分解的直和项, 矛盾。 因此$P_{c,a+2,a,b|a}$不是$\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_2)$的直和项。


Next, assume that $P_{c,a,d,b|a}$ is a direct summand. Then there exists a projective module $Q'$ such that
\begin{equation}\notag
  \begin{aligned}
    \prl(P_\lambda \otimes V_{2}) = Q' \oplus P_{c,a,d,b|a}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}

We also divide the argument into two cases.

Case 1. $d>a+2$.

Using the same weight $\nu$, we compute
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gl44-3}
  \begin{aligned}
    \pr_\nu(Q' \otimes V_{1}) &= 
    M_{a+2,b,c,d|a+1}
+ M_{d,c,b,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{c,d,a+2,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,a+2,b,d|a+1}
+ M_{a+2,c,d,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,b,d,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{d,a+2,c,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,d,b,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{d,c,a+2,b|a+1}
+ M_{a+2,c,b,d|a+1}
+ M_{d,b,c,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{c,a+2,d,b|a+1}
+ M_{c,b,a+2,d|a+1}
+ M_{a+2,d,c,b|a+1} \\
                             &=
\sum M_{a+2,b,c,d|a+1}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Again, $\nu$ is the minimal highest weight, and $\pr_\nu(Q' \otimes V_1)$ is projective, so $P_\nu$ should occur as a summand. However, $M_{c,a+2,d,b|a+1}$ has multiplicity one in \cref{eq:gl44-3} but multiplicity two in $\Filt(P_\nu)$, making such an embedding impossible. This contradiction excludes $P_{c,a,d,b|a}$ as well.

Case 2. $d=a+2$.

 直接计算$\pr_\nu(Q' \otimes V_1)$ 标准滤过如下
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gl44-6}
  \begin{aligned}
    \pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_{1}) &= 
2M_{a+2,b,c,a+2|a+1}
+2M_{a+2,c,b,a+2|a+1}
+3M_{c,a+2,a+2,b|a+1}
+2M_{c,a+2,b,a+2|a+1}
+2M_{a+2,c,a+2,b|a+1}
+2M_{c,b,a+2,a+2|a+1}
+2M_{a+2,a+2,c,b|a+1}
                             &=
2\sum M_{a+2,b,c,a+2|a+1}
+ M_{c,a+2,a+2,b|a+1}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}

通过公式\cref{eq:gl44-6},可以看出$\nu$是$\Filt(\pr_\nu(Q' \otimes V_{1}))$中的极小权，且$M_\nu$重数是2。这说明$\pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_{1})$中有两个$P_\nu$作为其分解的直和项。
但是，比较\cref{eq:gl44-6}和\cref{eq:gl44-5}，我们可知$\pr_\nu(Q' \otimes V_{1})$不可能有两个$P_\nu$作为其分解的直和项, 矛盾。 因此$P_{c,a,a+2,b|a}$不是$\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_2)$的直和项。

In conclusion, neither $P_{c,d,a,b|a}$ nor $P_{c,a,d,b|a}$ can occur as a direct summand of $\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_2)$.

根据前文的结论，我们可以有
\begin{equation}
  \begin{aligned}
    P_{a+2,a+2,a+1,a|a+2} &= 
M_{a+3,a+2,a+1,a|a+3}
+M_{a+2,a+3,a+1,a|a+3}
+M_{a+2,a+2,a+1,a|a+2}  \\
                          &=
 M_{a+2,a+2,a+1,a|a+2}
+\sum M_{a+2,a+3,a+1,a|a+3}
  \end{aligned}
  \label{eq:gl44-7}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}  \label{eq:gl44-8}
  \begin{aligned}
    P_{a+2,a+2,a,a+1|a+2}     &= 
    M_{a+3,a+2,a,a+1|a+3}
+ M_{a+2,a+3,a,a+1|a+3}
+ M_{a+2,a+2,a,a+1|a+2}
+ M_{a+3,a+2,a+1,a|a+3}
+ M_{a+2,a+3,a+1,a|a+3}
+ M_{a+2,a+2,a+1,a|a+2} \\
                                               &=
\sum M_{a+2,a+2,a,a+1|a+2}
+ \sum M_{a+2,a+3,a,a+1|a+3}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}

由于$\Filt(P_{a+2,a+2,a+1,a|a+2}) \in U$和$\Filt(P_{a+2,a+2,a,a+1|a+2}) \subseteq U$, 因此我们进行反证，分别排除它们作为$\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_{1})$ 直和项的可能

首先，假设$P_{a+2,a+2,a+1,a|a+2}$是$\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_{1})$的一个直和项，那么，存在一个投射模$Q$使得
\begin{equation}\notag
  \begin{aligned}
    \pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_{1}) = Q+ P_{a+2,a+2,a+1,a|a+2}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}

令$\nu = (a,a+4,a+2,a+1|a+3)$, 那么$\nu$是典型的权. 我们就有
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gl44-9}
  \begin{aligned}
    \pr_\nu ( Q \otimes V_1 ) &= 
M_{a+2,a+4,a,a+1|a+3}
+M_{a+4,a+1,a+2,a|a+3}
+M_{a+4,a,a+2,a+1|a+3}
+M_{a+4,a+2,a,a+1|a+3}
+M_{a+1,a+4,a+2,a|a+3}
+M_{a,a+4,a+2,a+1|a+3}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
观察到\cref{eq:gl44-9}中，权$\nu$在滤过中是极小的。$Q$是投射模，因此$ \pr_\nu ( Q \otimes V_1 )$也是投射模。根据\cref{lemma:2}, 表明$P_{\nu}$必然是$\pr_\nu ( Q \otimes V_1 )$的一个不可分解的直和项。但是\cref{thm:4}给出$P_\nu$的完整滤过的结构如下
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gl44-10}
  \begin{aligned}
    P_\nu &= 
    M_{a+2,a+4,a+1,a|a+3}
+M_{a+2,a+4,a,a+1|a+3}
+M_{a+4,a+1,a+2,a|a+3}
+M_{a+4,a,a+2,a+1|a+3}
+M_{a+4,a+2,a+1,a|a+3}
+M_{a+4,a+2,a,a+1|a+3}
+M_{a+1,a+4,a+2,a|a+3}
+M_{a,a+4,a+2,a+1|a+3} \\
          &=
\sum M_{a,a+4,a+2,a+1|a+3}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
比较\cref{eq:gl44-9}和\cref{eq:gl44-10}, 我们发现$P_\nu$不肯能是$\pr_\nu ( Q \otimes V_1 )$的直和项。矛盾，因此 排除了$P_{a+2,a+2,a+1,a|a+2}$作为$\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_{1})$ 直和项的可能. 同样使用$\nu$和$V_1$，也可以排除$P_{a+2,a+2,a,a+1|a+2}$作为$\pr_\lambda(P_\lambda \otimes V_{1})$ 直和项的可能.

综上$Pr_{\lambda}(P_\mu \otimes V_{1}) = P_\lambda  $.
