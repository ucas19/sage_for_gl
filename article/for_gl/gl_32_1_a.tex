The \cref{eq:4} reveals that the set $U$ contains all composition factors appearing in the standard filtration of $P_{a+2,a+2,a|a+1,a+2}$, and thus the possibility that $P_{a+2,a+2,a|a+1,a+2}$ occurs as a direct summand of $\prl(P_\mu \otimes V_3)$ cannot be ruled out by filtration comparison alone.

To resolve this indeterminacy, we employ a proof by contradiction. Suppose, for the sake of contradiction, that $P_{a+2,a+2,a|a+1,a+2}$ is indeed a direct summand of $\prl(P_\mu \otimes V_3)$. Then we may write
\begin{equation}\notag
  \begin{aligned}
\prl(P_\mu \otimes V_5) = Q \oplus P_{a+2,a+2,a|a+1,a+2}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
for some module $Q$. It suffices to show that $Q$ cannot be projective, which would contradict the fact that $\prl(P_\mu \otimes V_3)$ is a direct sum of projective modules. This contradiction will establish that $P_{a+2,a+2,a|a+1,a+2}$ is not a direct summand. Consequently, since all alternative projective summands have been systematically excluded, we conclude definitively that: $\prl(P_\mu \otimes V_3) = P_\lambda$.

Now, we shall show that $Q$ cannot be projective by contradiction.
Suppose, to the contrary, that $Q$ is projective. Then $pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_3)$ is also projective, and its standard filtration admits a decomposition into indecomposable projective summands.
Let $\nu = (a,a+3,a+3|a+1,a+2)$. We project $Q \otimes V_3$ on to the $\nu$ linkage block as follows.
\begin{equation}\notag
  \begin{aligned}
    pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_3) = 2M_{a+3,a,a+3|a+1,a+2} + 2M_{a,a+3,a+3|a+1,a+2} + M_{a+3,a+3,a|a+1,a+2}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Observe that the minimal weight among all composition factors in the standard filtration of $pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_3)$ is $\nu$. By \cref{lemma:2}, this implies that $pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_3)$ must contain at least two copies of $P_\nu$ as direct summands. However, a direct computation shows that
  \begin{equation}\notag
    \begin{aligned}
  P_\nu = M_{a+3,a,a+3|a+1,a+2} + M_{a,a+3,a+3|a+1,a+2} + M_{a+3,a+3,a|a+1,a+2}
    \end{aligned}
  \end{equation}
Consequently, $P_\nu$ cannot appear with multiplicity $\geq 2$, yielding a contradiction. Therefore, $Q$ cannot be projective.

To address this ambiguity, we directly apply the structural description of $P_{c+2,c+2,a|c+1,c+2}$ provided in \colorbox{orange}{Placeholder} as follows.
\begin{equation}\label{eq:6}
  \begin{aligned}

  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
We emphasize that this usage does not constitute circular reasoning, as \colorbox{orange}{Placeholder} is established independently of the current context.

The \cref{eq:6} reveals that the set $U$ contains all composition factors appearing in the standard filtration of $P_{c+2,c+2,a|c+1,c+2}$, and thus the possibility that $P_{c+2,c+2,a|c+1,c+2}$ occurs as a direct summand of $\prl(P_\mu \otimes V_2)$ cannot be ruled out by filtration comparison alone.

To resolve this indeterminacy, we employ a proof by contradiction. Suppose, for the sake of contradiction, that $P_{c+2,c+2,a|c+1,c+2}$ is indeed a direct summand of $\prl(P_\mu \otimes V_2)$. Then we may write
\begin{equation}\notag
  \begin{aligned}
\prl(P_\mu \otimes V_2) = Q \oplus P_{c+2,c+2,a|c+1,c+2}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
for some module $Q$. It suffices to show that $Q$ cannot be projective, which would contradict the fact that $\prl(P_\mu \otimes V_3)$ is a direct sum of projective modules. This contradiction will establish that $P_{c+2,c+2,a|c+1,c+2}$ is not a direct summand. Consequently, since all alternative projective summands have been systematically excluded, we conclude definitively that: $\prl(P_\mu \otimes V_2) = P_\lambda$.

Now, we shall show that $Q$ cannot be projective by contradiction.
Suppose, to the contrary, that $Q$ is projective. Then $pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_3)$ is also projective, and its standard filtration admits a decomposition into indecomposable projective summands.
Let $\nu = (a,c+3,c+3|c+1,c+2)$. We project $Q \otimes V_3$ on to the $\nu$ linkage block as follows.
\begin{equation}\notag
  \begin{aligned}
    pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_3) = 2M_{a,c+3,c+3|c+1,c+2} + 2M_{c+3,a,c+3|c+1,c+2} + M_{c+3,c+3,a|c+1,c+2}
  \end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Observe that the minimal weight among all composition factors in the standard filtration of $pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_3)$ is $\nu$. By \cref{lemma:2}, this implies that $pr_\nu(Q \otimes V_3)$ must contain at least two copies of $P_\nu$ as direct summands. However, a direct computation shows that
  \begin{equation}\notag
    \begin{aligned}
  P_\nu = M_{a,c+3,c+3|c+1,c+2} + M_{c+3,a,c+3|c+1,c+2} + M_{c+3,c+3,a|c+1,c+2}
    \end{aligned}
  \end{equation}
Consequently, $P_\nu$ cannot appear with multiplicity $\geq 2$, yielding a contradiction. Therefore, $Q$ cannot be projective.
